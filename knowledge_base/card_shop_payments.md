# Card payments in shops

Fictional Demo Finance policy for a local portfolio demonstration.

## Chip and PIN payment declined

1. Describe the error with sensitive details removed and whether the problem also occurs at another terminal. Do not ask again for details already supplied.
2. Check in the app whether the card is activated or frozen and whether sufficient funds are available.
3. After repeated PIN errors, stop retrying and contact support. A decline alone cannot prove insufficient funds, an incorrect PIN or a merchant fault; do not guess the cause.

## Contactless payment not working

1. After activation, make one shop payment by inserting the card into the terminal and entering your PIN before trying contactless. If chip and PIN already works, use one inserted-card payment and then retry contactless.
2. If contactless still fails at different terminals, check the contactless setting in the app and contact support.
3. A single failed tap does not prove that the card is damaged or blocked.
