# Fictional card support knowledge base

These manually authored policies describe **Demo Finance**, an invented institution.
They are portfolio demonstration material, not real banking policy or financial advice.
All time estimates, app screens, and procedures are fictional. Replies are
previewed automatically; every reply needs a support worker's review before use.

Scope: physical card delivery, activation, and cards not working in shops or online.
The application has no customer accounts, delivery records, transactions, or balances.
General policy cannot establish the status of an individual customer's case.
Loan decisions, investment advice, transfers, and insurance coverage are outside scope.

Each policy uses a title, a fictional-policy notice, and short `##` sections. A whole
section is one chunk; the current reader supports this deliberately simple format.
Do not add code fences containing `##` headings or deeply nested policy structures.
Keep sections focused and short rather than splitting instructions mid-sentence.
The title and introductory notice are metadata and are not indexed. This README is
also excluded from the search index. The interface labels the corpus fictional.

Chunk identifiers combine the filename and section heading. Renaming either changes
the identifier and requires updating development expectations and rerunning evaluation.
Search results also include the relative filename and one-based source line range.
