# Physical card activation

Fictional Demo Finance policy for a local portfolio demonstration.

## Activate a physical card

1. After your physical card arrives, sign in to the app. Open Cards, select the matching physical card and choose Activate. Follow the app's confirmation instructions.
2. After activation, make your first shop payment by inserting the card into the payment terminal and entering your PIN. Do this before trying contactless payments.
3. Never share your full card number, PIN, password or verification code in a support message. The assistant cannot activate your card or verify its activation status.

## Activation error

1. Check that you selected the physical card that arrived. Update the app and retry on a stable internet connection.
2. If the error persists, contact support with the exact error message, after removing sensitive details. If the error has not been described, ask for that message without sensitive information.
3. Do not assume that activation succeeded. The assistant cannot change account status. If the card is already activated but fails at a terminal, use payment troubleshooting instead of activating it again.
