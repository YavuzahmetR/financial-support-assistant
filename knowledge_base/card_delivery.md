# Physical card delivery

Fictional Demo Finance policy for a local portfolio demonstration.

## Delivery estimate

1. A new physical card normally arrives in 5 to 8 business days after dispatch, not after ordering. Weekends and public holidays do not count as business days.
2. This is an estimate, not a guaranteed arrival date. Establish the dispatch date before deciding whether delivery is late.
3. General guidance cannot prove that your card has been dispatched or will arrive on a particular day.

## Tracking and late delivery

1. In the app, open Cards, select the ordered card and choose Delivery. A tracking link appears only if the courier supplies one.
2. If the card has not arrived after 8 business days from dispatch, check the delivery address in the app and contact support for a delivery review. If dispatch timing is unknown, ask for it before deciding that delivery is late.
3. Do not promise a refund, replacement or new arrival date before review. Only an authorized delivery record can confirm the courier location or shipment status; this demo cannot access that record.
