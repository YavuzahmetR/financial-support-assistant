# Damaged physical cards

Fictional Demo Finance policy for a local portfolio demonstration.

## Damaged card replacement

1. If your card is cracked, bent or has a damaged chip, avoid forcing it into a payment terminal.
2. In the app, open Cards, select the damaged card and choose Replace card to view your replacement options.
3. Review any displayed cost before confirming. This policy does not define fees: do not quote a price or promise a free replacement.
4. The assistant cannot order a replacement or check whether you have already ordered one.

## Card problem details needed

1. Describe whether the problem occurs with contactless, chip and PIN, or online payments. Give the error message with sensitive details removed.
2. Say whether the card is new and whether it appears frozen in the app. Never send sensitive card details.
3. This context helps choose troubleshooting steps. It does not prove the cause of the problem or authorize an account change.
